Get to Know Us

Eden Farm is a trusted producer of premium fruits, vegetables, mushrooms and honey — supplying supermarkets, restaurants, hotels and local markets since 2002. What began as a small family-run farm has grown into a diversified agricultural operation driven by innovation, sustainability and consistency.

In 2017, we expanded our offering to include farm-fresh mushrooms using advanced Dutch growing technology, adding white button, brown and portobello varieties to our already wide range of produce such as melons, cucumbers, tomatoes, cauliflower, broccoli, peppers, eggplant, lettuce, herbs, green beans and more.
